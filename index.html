Minecraft 101

October 6, 2026 by Rene Jones

Let's talk Minecraft. Now, if you've ended up on this blog, I assume you are already plenty familiar with Minecraft, however, on the off chance that you aren't, here is everthing you need to know.

Minecraft is a sandbox and (sometimes) survial video game developed by Mojang Studios. It is available for play on personal computers, mobile phones and video game consoles. Minecraft has three-dimensional graphics, with each object showing clear composition of blocks. Much of the gameplay revolves around breaking these blocks ("mining") to then build their own structures, tools and weapons. Minecraft has two primary game modes: Survival Mode and Creative Mode. In survival mode, the player is expected to collect sufficient food, garner shelter and battle mobs in order to survive. In creative mode, the player's character does not die, instead the player roams the open world freely and flexes their creativity (get it) to build anything- simple or elaborate- for the entire duration of their gameplay.
